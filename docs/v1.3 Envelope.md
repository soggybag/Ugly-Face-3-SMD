## Why the v1.1 envelope is weak

- The vactrol LED is fed from **PRE_OUT** (R7 100 Ω → C8 100µ → ENVELOPE RV1 B1k → LED), the ×201 preamp **after it clips**. Every note is the same ~7 Vpp square, so loudness information is mostly gone: what's left is how long a note keeps clipping.
- The LED only conducts on the positive half-cycle, and only above its ~1.8 V forward voltage. With the pot as a divider, nothing happens below ~50 % of the rotation.
- Bench tests (2026-10-15/16) of rewiring RV1 as a rheostat, with a diode or a 2k2 for C8's DC return: too strong or too weak. The drive itself has to change. Full background: `../Ugly Face Roadmap.md` → *Envelope: string balance*.

## New circuit (roadmap option A, simplified for UF3)

UF3 already has a **clean** copy of the guitar: **DRY_OUT** (U4A, ×4.3). The new envelope is taken from it, so the preamp and gate are untouched. One **LM358** and one transistor are added.

```
DRY_OUT ─ C20 33n ─ R26 10k ─┬─ RV1 pin 3          ENVELOPE = A10k level control (pin 1 to VB)
                             RV1 (A10k)             tilt: 33n with 20k → ~240 Hz high-pass
                     VB ─────┴─ RV1 pin 1           (evens out low E vs thin strings)
                 RV1 wiper ──► U5A +  (pin 3)

U5A: peak detector with gain ×22
   out (pin 1) ─ D5 1N4148W ─ R27 1k ─┬─ ENV ── C21 1µ ─ GND        attack ~1 ms
                                      ├─ R28 470k ─ U5A − (pin 2) ── R29 22k ─ VB
                                      └─ R30 22k ─ RV6 500k trim ─ VB        DECAY ~20–250 ms

U5B + Q1: current sink for the vactrol LED, I_LED ≈ 2.1 × (ENV − VB − 0.2 V) / 330 Ω
   ENV ─ U5B + (pin 5)
   U5B − (pin 6) ── R31 100k ─ +9V,  R36 1M ─ +9V,  R32 100k ─ Q1 emitter
   U5B out (pin 7) ─ R33 1k ─ Q1 base (MMBT3904)
   Q1 emitter ─ R34 330 ─ GND
   +9V ─ R35 100 ─ vactrol LED anode (U3 pin 1);  LED cathode (U3 pin 2) ─ Q1 collector
```

### How it works

- **Tilt:** C20 / R26 + RV1 form a ~240 Hz high-pass. That takes ~8–10 dB off the low E fundamental, so the thin strings drive the envelope about as hard as the low E. The 10k / 10k divider also halves the signal, keeping U5A's input inside the LM358's range on hard peaks.
- **ENVELOPE (RV1)** is now a level control in front of the detector. **Fully counter-clockwise = no envelope at all**; the A-taper gives an even feel across the rotation.
- **U5A** is a peak detector with gain (×22). It charges C21 quickly (R27 1k, ~1 ms) and **DECAY** (R30 + RV6 trimmer) lets it fall back to VB. Fast, short notes still register.
- **U5B + Q1** is a voltage-to-current converter. It compares ENV with +9V / 2 (ratiometric, the same as VB) and sinks LED current through Q1. A current drive has no 1.8 V dead zone, so small envelopes still light the LED.
  - **R36 1M** makes a deliberate ~0.2 V dead zone, so the LED is **fully off at rest** even with the LM358's worst-case offset (±7 mV × 22 gain).
- **Current limit:** the LM358's output tops out ~1.5 V below +9V, and Q1 runs out of headroom, so the LED current can't exceed ~15 mA. That's safe for the vactrol LED.
- **Bonus:** PRE_OUT no longer drives the LED, so the gate signal is cleaner.

### Simulation (`sim/envelope_v13.py`, ngspice)

- **Rest, no input:** LED current **0** with detector offset 0, +7 mV and −7 mV.
- **Decaying note at DRY_OUT** (peak LED mA / LED mA after 1.4 s):

| DRY_OUT peak (≈ guitar) | ENVELOPE max | ENVELOPE ~noon | ENVELOPE low |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.2 V (~50 mV, soft), low E / high E | 3.7 / 9.0 mA | 0 / 1.0 | 0 / 0 |
| 1 V (~230 mV, medium) | 14.6 / 14.6 | 4.7 / 11.6 | 0 / 1.0 |
| 3 V (~700 mV, hard) | 14.8 / 14.7 | 13.8 / 14.6 | 2.7 / 6.5 |

- The full ENVELOPE rotation is useful, and fully down really is off.
- **Tilt:** with sine tests the high E gets about 2× the low E. Real low E notes are louder (pickup output) and rich in harmonics, so on a guitar they should come out roughly even. **Tune on the breadboard:** C20 33n → 68n is gentler, 22n is stronger.

### MIX: would a tapered pot help?

- MIX (RV4 B10k) is a pan pot: each end shunts one side to VB.
- A taper doesn't change the levels at the two ends. It only moves **where** in the rotation the balance falls, and it crowds the other half of the travel.
- The imbalance is a level difference (and a square wave sounds louder than its level suggests), so the **resistor is the right fix**: R13 220k → 330k (or 470k). Keep RV4 B10k.
- Tack a 330k (or a 100k in series with the 220k) on unit 1 and listen. If noon sounds balanced, that's the v1.3 value.

## Next step: bench-test on unit 1 before the PCB

Build the new envelope on a small breadboard and wire it to the v1.1 board:

1. **Taps from the board:** **+9V** (C19 +, or U4 pin 8), **GND**, **VB** (U4 pin 7), **DRY_OUT** (U4 pin 1).
2. **Vactrol:** unsolder the LED leads (U3 pads 1 and 2) and run them to the breadboard (anode → R35, cathode → Q1 collector). **Leave the LDR on pads 3 and 4.**
3. Remove **R7** (or C8), so PRE_OUT no longer feeds the old LED path. The board's RV1 then does nothing; use an A10k (or any 10k) on the breadboard as ENVELOPE.
4. **Checks:**
   - At rest: ENV (C21) ≈ VB, LED current (V across R34 ÷ 330) ≈ 0, LED dark.
   - Pluck the low E and high E: watch ENV; the peaks should be similar.
   - Listen: ENVELOPE range, DECAY trimmer range vs the LDR's own lag, low E vs thin strings.
   - Try C20 22n / 33n / 68n.

Through-hole equivalents for the breadboard: LM358 DIP, 2N3904, 1N4148, ¼ W resistors.
